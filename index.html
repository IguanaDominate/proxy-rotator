Features

Everything you need for a clean proxy pool

From discovery to rotation — proxy-rotator handles the full lifecycle of your scraping proxies.

🧭

Multi-Protocol Generation

Generate HTTP, HTTPS and SOCKS5 proxies from a single panel with configurable source lists and endpoint ranges.

⚡

Bulk Checker

Validate thousands of proxies in parallel with adjustable timeouts, retries and concurrency to keep only working endpoints.

🌍

Geo Filter

Filter results by country or region before rotation so your scrapers always route through the locations you target.

🔄

Smart Rotation

Rotate proxies per request, per session or on a timer with automatic failover when an endpoint drops.

📤

Live JSON / TXT Export

Push the active pool to JSON or TXT in real time — drop it straight into Scrapy, Puppeteer or your own stack.

🖥️

Native Windows App

A lightweight desktop client for Windows 10 and 11 — no Docker, no dependencies, just launch the executable.
